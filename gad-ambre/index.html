Amber resin, oud and a thread of smoke. It starts the way the materials arrive: in a walnut box.

The box

Open it
slowly

Oiled walnut, brass hinges, a lid that takes its time. The flacon ships in it, and the box stays on the shelf long after.

The resins

Amber, in
its rough form

Labdanum and benzoin arrive like this: sticky, uneven, smelling of warm stone. We melt them down for the base instead of using a ready amber accord.

The flacon

Heavy glass,
a ribbed cap

Thick rounded glass that holds the colour of the juice, and a ribbed metal cap that closes with a click.
