How we collect

Slow
on purpose

Four steps from the flower to the jar, and none of them is a machine that speeds things up.

  1. Hives go to the bloomWe move the hives to the field when it flowers, not the other way round.
  2. Only capped combA frame comes out when the bees have sealed it. Then the honey is ripe and under 18% water.
  3. Cold extractionSpun out of the comb, strained through a fine mesh, never warmed above the temperature of the hive.
  4. Jarred by handGlass jars, field and week on the label, every batch checked in a lab before it ships.
“We don’t make honey. We carry the hives and wait.”

Visitors are welcome on summer weekends: veil on, smoker lit, a spoon from the frame.
