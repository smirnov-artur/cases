01 · The hall

Glass on
every side

Forty metres of curved glass, a white marble floor and a chandelier of three thousand crystals. At sunset the whole room turns gold.

02 · Weddings

The first dance
is yours

We clear the floor, dim the chandelier and let the sun do the lighting. One song, and the whole hall is only for the two of you.

03 · The floor

200 guests,
one floor

The floor holds sixty couples at once. Tables stand along the glass, so nobody dines with their back to the view.

04 · After dark

The sky
dims the lights

After nine the sky goes violet, then deep blue. We bring the light down with it, and the stars come out over the clouds.

05 · The last dance

The last waltz
by the window

The band plays one more waltz. The last light sits on the clouds, and the evening ends where it began: at the glass.
